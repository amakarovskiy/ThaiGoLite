Driving Rules in Phuket

Left-hand trafficHelmet mandatoryZero alcohol

Phuket’s main risks are unfamiliar left-hand traffic, wet markings, sand, sudden lane changes and mixed traffic speeds. A valid motorcycle entitlement, helmets and sobriety are essential; never plan a route around avoiding checks.

01

Basic Traffic Rules

Keep left. Take extra care when moving off after a stop and when turning at junctions: right-hand-traffic habit causes many mistakes there.

Follow road signs and posted speed limits. Slow before bends, on wet or dirty surfaces and in dense traffic; leave space to brake.

Do not cross solid lines to overtake, sit in truck blind spots or assume others will yield. Use the vehicle’s standard lights as required by the bike and road conditions.

02

Documents and police checks

Police may stop a rider and check the motorcycle entitlement, IDP or Thai licence, helmets and vehicle papers. Keep the contract and supplied document pack accessible.

If an offence is recorded, follow official instructions and request an official receipt or written confirmation. Do not offer informal payment or negotiate a “deal”.

Do not choose routes to avoid enforcement. The sound strategy is the correct licence, two helmets, a roadworthy bike, sobriety and compliance with signs.

03

The essentials

Helmets

A properly fastened helmet is required for the rider and one passenger. Three people on one bike is not permitted.

Alcohol

The safest clear rule: if you drink, do not ride. Alcohol sharply raises risk and may exclude insurance cover.

Licence and papers

Carry a valid motorcycle licence, the corresponding IDP when required, the contract and the bike documents.

Rain and sand

Slow before the manoeuvre, brake smoothly and avoid abrupt lean on wet paint or sand.

04

If a crash happens

Safety first: stop, prevent a second collision, call ambulance 1669 for injuries and police 191 when required.

Contact ThaiGo immediately: photograph and video the location, vehicles, damage and participant details. Do not sign what you do not understand or settle without a record.

The next steps and insurance decision depend on the contract, policy, rider licence, circumstances and official documents. We cannot promise approval or zero cost in advance.

Choosing a suitable model

A light scooter is easier to hold but does not replace experience. Choose only within your licence and skills; a passenger needs a proper seat, footrests and a second helmet. Without road experience, a car or taxi is safer.
