FAQ

How much does it cost to rent a bike?

Honda Scoopy from 130 ฿/day, Honda Click from 130 ฿/day, PCX/NMAX from 250 ฿/day. Longer rental — lower price.

What documents do I need?

Passport and a valid motorcycle entitlement: a Thai motorcycle licence, or a national category A licence with the corresponding IDP when required. We check it and record its details in the contract. The cash deposit starts at 3,000 ฿; your original passport stays with you.

Is there insurance?

The rental includes basic accident-medical cover up to 30,000 ฿, strictly subject to the policy terms and exclusions. This is not comprehensive cover: damage to or theft of the bike and third-party loss may remain the renter's responsibility under the contract. We show the terms before signing.

Is it safe to ride?

Traffic keeps left. Rider and passenger must wear properly fastened helmets; follow road signs and posted speed limits. Do not ride after drinking alcohol. Violations may lead to a fine and may affect insurance cover.

How much is fuel?

~43 ฿/liter. Scooter fuel use — 2 L/100 km. A 50 km trip costs ~45 ฿ in fuel.
