Bike Rental Insurance in Phuket

Policy termsNot comprehensiveCheck before signing

The word “insurance” does not mean everything is covered. The current contract, policy, rider licence, incident facts and exclusions decide the outcome. Check the following before accepting the bike.

01

Basic cover included with the rental

ThaiGo includes basic accident-medical cover up to 30,000 ฿. The limit, beneficiaries, required documents, medical providers and exclusions are defined by the current policy.

This is not comprehensive motor cover and does not replace your own travel medical insurance. Treatment above the limit, evacuation, repatriation and other costs may be excluded.

Before signing, ask to see the policy and contract terms. Keep copies and the contact details needed after an incident.

02

What may remain your responsibility

Bike damage or theft, third-party property, injury claims and loss of use are not automatically covered. Read the exact contract and policy wording.

The site does not promise a separate Insurance+ package or a fixed damage cap for motorcycles. Any extra protection applies only when expressly stated in your current quote and signed contract.

Wrong or missing motorcycle entitlement, alcohol, an undeclared rider, prohibited use or late reporting may lead to refusal or limitation of cover.

03

Cover at a glance

Medical costs

Up to 30,000 ฿ only within the current policy, its limits, required documents and exclusions.

Bike damage

Do not treat it as covered without an express clause in the signed contract. Handover and return photos are important evidence.

Third parties

Do not assume automatic cover for other vehicles, property or every claim made by an injured person.

Licence and conditions

Correct entitlement, sobriety, declared rider and compliance with the incident procedure are key review conditions.

04

What to do after an incident

1. Make the scene safe. Call 1669 for injuries and police 191 when required.

2. Contact ThaiGo immediately. Do not leave or repair the bike without agreement unless safety requires it.

3. Record evidence. Photograph the location, vehicles, plates, damage, road conditions and participant details.

4. Follow the written procedure. Obtain the police and medical documents required by the contract or insurer.

Coverage is decided after the documents and circumstances are reviewed. A payout, deposit return or fixed contribution cannot be promised in advance.

What to check for every model

Before paying, verify the insurer or programme, limit, deductible, exclusions, bike and third-party cover, licence requirements and post-crash procedure. If it is not written into the contract, do not treat a verbal statement as an insurance guarantee.
