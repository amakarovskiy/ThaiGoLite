FAQ

What is Yamaha MT-15's Dark Fighter design and what makes it appealing?

Dark Fighter is Yamaha's signature design philosophy for the MT series: aggressive angles, dual LED headlights resembling a predator's eyes, and a minimalist silhouette. It looks like a scaled-down version of the legendary MT-09, attracting those who love bold styling.

What advantage does the MT-15's light 132 kg weight give when riding Phuket?

At 132 kg, it's the lightest manual motorcycle in our fleet, delivering instant steering response and effortless maneuvering in traffic. On the narrow streets of Patong or Phuket Town, this lightness is priceless — you feel complete control.

Why is the MT-15 considered the most aggressive-looking among 155cc bikes?

Among 155cc motorcycles, it stands out with its transformer-like design — sharp lines, exposed engine, and predatory face have no rivals in the class. It's a motorcycle for those who want to turn heads with looks, not just speed.
