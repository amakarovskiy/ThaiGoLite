How to Rent a Bike in Phuket

~ 30 minutesNo passport depositContract included

A passport alone is not enough: you need a valid motorcycle entitlement. Before handover, we record the documents, bike condition, price, deposit and liability terms in the rental contract.

01

What You Need

Bring your passport and a valid motorcycle entitlement: a Thai motorcycle licence, or a national category A licence with the corresponding IDP when required for your licence. It must remain valid for the whole rental.

We check the licence and record its number, issue date and expiry in the contract. We do not hand over a bike without the appropriate entitlement. Police may request the documents; a violation may lead to a fine and insurance problems.

Minimum age is 18. You need real two-wheel experience in road traffic. We explain the controls of the specific model, but we do not provide first-rider training on public roads.

02

The Process

Step 1: message us on WhatsApp or Telegram with dates, number of riders, area and experience. We suggest models that match the licence and planned route.

Step 2: agree the price, deposit, delivery and return. Delivery availability and cost depend on area and time; we confirm them in writing before payment.

Step 3: we check the passport and licence, inspect and photograph the bike together, then sign a contract stating price, term, insurance, liability and licence details. Your original passport stays with you.

03

Contract, payment and insurance

Deposit

The amount depends on the model and is stated in the current quote and contract. We do not keep your original passport as security.

Payment

Method, amount and timing are confirmed during booking. Keep the contract and proof of payment.

Contract

The contract records the vehicle, term, price, deposit, condition, licence and each party’s responsibility. Read it before key handover.

Insurance

Basic accident-medical cover is up to 30,000 ฿ and only under the policy terms. It is not comprehensive cover.

04

What is included — and what is not

Normally included: two helmets, handover inspection and photos, contract, basic cover within the policy, and help with a breakdown not caused by the renter.

Do not assume these are covered: bike damage or theft, third-party loss, traffic violations, fuel, or your own travel/medical insurance. The contract and policy define the exact scope.

At return, condition, fuel and supplied items are checked against the handover record. Any extra charge must have a contractual basis and supporting evidence.

Choosing a model for an experienced rider

A light scooter does not turn the road into a training ground. We match the model to your licence, real experience, height, passenger and route. If you have never ridden on two wheels, choose a car or taxi instead.
