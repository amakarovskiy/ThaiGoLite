FAQ

What's different about the Toyota Yaris 2025 compared to the 2023 model?

The Yaris 2025 features a refreshed design, an upgraded infotainment system with a larger screen, and more advanced safety systems. Fuel economy stays at an efficient 5.5 L/100 km, but the driving experience feels noticeably more modern.

What safety features does the Yaris 2025 have?

The Yaris 2025 includes Toyota Safety Sense: automatic emergency braking, lane departure alert, and adaptive cruise control. For unfamiliar Phuket roads, these features add real peace of mind.

Is it worth paying more for the Yaris 2025 over the 2023?

If you value the latest tech, a bigger screen, and advanced safety — the premium is justified. If you just need a reliable car to get around, the Yaris 2023 does the job perfectly at a lower price.
